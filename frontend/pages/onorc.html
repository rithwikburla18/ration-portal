Important: This portal demonstrates the citizen-side ONORC workflow. Actual foodgrain release requires the authorised ePoS process and applicable beneficiary authentication at the Fair Price Shop.

How ONORC Works

1. Existing Card Use the same NFSA ration card issued by your Home State / UT.
2. Choose Destination Select the State / UT and District where you currently need ration.
3. Visit FPS Approach an ePoS-enabled Fair Price Shop at the destination.
4. Authentication & Entitlement The authorised ePoS process retrieves entitlement information and completes the portability transaction.

No New Ration Card

ONORC is designed so eligible beneficiaries can use their existing NFSA ration card instead of obtaining a new ration card in the destination State / UT.

For Migrant Beneficiaries

The service is particularly useful for beneficiaries who temporarily move to another State / UT for employment or other reasons.
